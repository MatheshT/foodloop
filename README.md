# 🔄 FoodLoop — Next-Generation Surplus Food Redistribution Network

> **Zero Hunger (UN SDG 2) • Climate Action (UN SDG 13) • Responsible Consumption (UN SDG 12)**

FoodLoop connects restaurants, hostels, banquet caterers, and supermarkets with verified shelters and community food banks through AI food vision, HACCP temperature telemetry, dynamic proximity matching, and audit-grade ESG accounting.

---

## 🌟 Key Capabilities & Next-Gen Innovations

### 1. 🤖 Deep AI Implementation
- **AI Computer Vision Food Scanner Studio**:
  - Upload real kitchen photos or test preset culinary samples.
  - Interactive **Thermal Infrared Heatmap Simulation**: visualizes simulated core holding temperatures.
  - **Neural Optical Ingredients Breakdown**: detects carbohydrates, protein ratios, and vegetables with percentage confidence.
  - **Estimated Nutritional Profile**: computes calories, proteins, carbohydrates, fats, and dietary fibers per serving.
  - **Microbial Pathogen & Spoilage Evaluation**: verifies safe pathogen thresholds before dispatch.
- **Embedded LoopAI Kitchen Copilot**:
  - Conversational AI assistant supporting donors with HACCP holding procedures, safe packaging, legal safe-harbor protections (Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Act / FSSAI directives), and corporate tax deduction calculations.

### 2. 🏢 Enhanced Donor Command Hub (Bento Grid)
- **Donor Lifetime Impact Dashboard**: Real-time display of total meals served (4,820+), CSR tax value earned ($16,870+), and Tier 1 Platinum badge.
- **Corporate Tax & CSR Savings Estimator**: Calculates enhanced deductions under US IRC §170(e)(3) and Section 80G with live meal-volume and cost-basis sliders.
- **Automated Recurring Surplus Dispatcher**: Set recurring daily or weekend schedules (e.g. daily lunch surplus at 14:30) so couriers are pre-staged automatically.

### 3. 🗺️ Multi-Objective Proximity & Urgency Matching Engine
- Computes multi-factor scores considering distance, remaining safe consumption window, shelter intake capacity, and cold-chain storage compatibility.

### 4. 🌡️ Digital HACCP & Automated Quarantine Protocol
- Enforces FDA/FSSAI safe holding standards ($\ge 60^\circ\text{C}$ for hot-hold, $\le 4^\circ\text{C}$ for cold-chain). Compromised batches are automatically diverted to biogas digesters.

### 5. 🔐 Cryptographic Dual-Handshake Chain of Custody
- 4-Digit Donor Pickup OTP + Recipient Rotating QR Token ensure zero fraudulent claims or misallocations.

### 6. 🌱 Audit-Grade ESG & Carbon Offset Ledger
- Computed according to WRI & UNEP GHG Protocol standards ($2.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e/kg}$, $840\text{ L water/kg}$). Generates printable official certificates with cryptographic verification seals.

## 🚀 Effortless 1-Click Startup

### Option A: Double-Click (Easiest for Windows)
Simply double-click:
📂 **`start.bat`** in the `foodloop` folder.  
It will automatically launch your browser at **http://localhost:3001** and start the FoodLoop production server!

### Option B: Terminal Command
```bash
cd foodloop
npm start
```

Visit the application in your browser:
👉 **http://localhost:3001** (or **http://localhost:5173**)
